Privacy Policy
Night Shift - Sokoban Puzzle

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This policy explains what the Night Shift app does with your information. The
short version: the game stores your progress on your own device, and the only
data leaving your phone is what Google's advertising service collects in order
to show ads.


INFORMATION WE COLLECT OURSELVES

None. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server of ours. We do not collect
your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, or files.


INFORMATION STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

The app saves the following locally, and it never leaves your phone except in a
device backup you control:

- Bays cleared, current streak, and hint tokens
- Your music, sound, and vibration settings
- The date you last played the daily bay

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.


ADVERTISING

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. To do that, AdMob collects and processes
certain data, which may include:

- Your device's advertising ID
- IP address, device model, operating system version, and coarse location
  derived from the IP address
- Ad interaction events, such as whether an ad was viewed or tapped

This collection is performed by Google, not by us, and is governed by Google's
own policies:

Google Privacy Policy - https://policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses data from AdMob - https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543


YOUR CHOICES ABOUT ADS

Consent. If you are in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, the app shows a consent
form on first launch using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change your
choice later from the app's privacy options where that control is offered.

Reset or delete your advertising ID. Android Settings > Privacy > Ads lets you
reset the identifier or delete it entirely.

Rewarded ads are optional. Videos that grant hint tokens are only shown after
you choose to watch one.


CHILDREN

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
data from them. If you believe a child has provided information through this
app, contact us and we will address it.


NETWORK USE

The puzzles themselves are generated on your device and the game is playable
without a connection. A connection is required to load the next bay so that an
advertisement can be served.


CHANGES

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address
with a new "last updated" date.
